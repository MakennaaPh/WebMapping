Cartography & Remote Sensing

Cartographic Design & Remote Sensing Analysis

A collection of advanced cartographic layouts, remote sensing analyses, and GIS modeling projects.

Mount St. Helens Ecological Recovery

NDVI differencing and change detection matrices analyzing vegetative regrowth and landscape evolution between 1979 and 1985 following the volcanic eruption.

GEDI Waveform Biophysical Metrics

Analysis of LiDAR GEDI waveform data across Redwood forest ecosystems, evaluating canopy height, plant area index (PAI), and terrain metrics.

Sentinel-2 Water Vapor & Rectification

Multispectral image analysis using Sentinel-2 imagery, exploring atmospheric correction, band ratios, and polynomial rectification resampling techniques.

Cartographic Standards

All maps are produced following rigorous cartographic principles in ArcGIS Pro and QGIS—incorporating clear visual hierarchy, balanced color theory (ivory, deep plum, and natural muted tones), precise scale representation, and accessible typography.
